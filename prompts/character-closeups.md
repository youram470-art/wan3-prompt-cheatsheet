# Character close-ups

Eight templates for faces, hands and small human beats. All of them assume a locked-off camera;
add a move from `landscape-camera-moves.md` only if the shot needs one.

---

### 01 · Window light portrait, 50mm

```
Close-up of {subject}, seated beside a large window, soft overcast daylight falling from camera
left, 50mm lens at f/2, shallow depth of field, visible skin texture, {mood} expression, slow
breathing motion only, camera locked off, 24 fps, fine film grain.
```

**Slots** — `{subject}` person + age + one detail · `{mood}` calm / wary / relieved
**Use when** you need a human beat before a reveal and cannot afford a distracting move.

---

### 02 · Night portrait, practical light

```
Medium close-up of {subject} standing under a single sodium street lamp, warm orange light from
above and slightly behind, cool blue spill from a shop window on the cheek, 85mm equivalent,
background falling into black, slight head turn toward camera, shallow breathing, handheld
micro-shake, 24 fps.
```

**Slots** — `{subject}` · optionally `{palette}` to swap sodium for neon magenta
**Use when** the scene is night and you want the face readable without a fill light.

---

### 03 · Hands, tabletop detail

```
Extreme close-up of {subject}'s hands, resting on a worn wooden table, fingers slowly uncurling,
one cut on the knuckle, soft top light with a hard falloff, 100mm macro, shallow focus on the
index finger, dust visible in the air, static camera, {palette}.
```

**Slots** — `{subject}` · `{palette}` muted browns / cold grey
**Use when** you need tension without showing a face.

---

### 04 · Over-the-shoulder, shallow focus

```
Over-the-shoulder shot of {subject} looking at {object} on a desk, foreground shoulder soft and
out of focus, focus pulling from the shoulder to the object over three seconds, warm desk lamp
from the right, 35mm at f/1.8, no camera movement, 24 fps.
```

**Slots** — `{subject}` · `{object}` a phone / a letter / a photograph
**Use when** the audience must read a prop, and the face only has to react.

---

### 05 · Profile in a moving car

```
Profile close-up of {subject} in the passenger seat of a moving car at dusk, street lights
sweeping across the face in soft bands, reflections sliding across the window glass, 50mm, f/2,
the horizon blurred behind, camera locked to the car interior, gentle vertical road vibration,
24 fps.
```

**Slots** — `{subject}`
**Use when** a character is thinking and the shot needs movement that does not come from the camera.

---

### 06 · Candle-lit interior

```
Close-up of {subject}, candlelight from below the chin, warm 1800K flame flicker on the skin,
deep shadows behind, 65mm, f/1.4, subtle involuntary micro-movements in the face, very slight
camera drift to the right, 24 fps, soft halation on highlights.
```

**Slots** — `{subject}` · `{mood}` frightened / resolute
**Use when** the setting is pre-electric and the mood needs to be uneasy.

---

### 07 · Sweat and breath, post-effort

```
Tight close-up of {subject} after physical effort, sweat on the temple and upper lip, chest
rising with heavy breathing, hair stuck to the forehead, hard side light from a doorway, 85mm,
f/2.8, background dark and flat, camera static, 24 fps, high micro-contrast.
```

**Slots** — `{subject}`
**Use when** you need exhaustion to read in two seconds without dialogue.

---

### 08 · Two-shot, one speaking

```
Two-shot of {subject_a} and {subject_b} facing each other at close distance, {subject_a} speaking
with small head movements, {subject_b} listening and blinking, soft window light from behind
camera, 40mm, f/2.8, both faces in focus, no camera movement, 24 fps.
```

**Slots** — `{subject_a}`, `{subject_b}` · keep both descriptions under 8 words each
**Use when** the beat is a conversation but you only need one line of dialogue.

---

**Tip** — for any close-up, render 3 seconds and cut. Faces drift in longer renders faster than
any other subject; the third second is usually where the eyes start to lose focus.
