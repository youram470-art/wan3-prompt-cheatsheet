# Landscape and camera moves

Eight templates for establishing shots and movement. Each one names the move, its speed and
where it ends — a shot description without an end point gives you a move that drifts.

---

### 09 · Drone reveal over a valley

```
Aerial shot starting tight on {landmark}, drone rising and pulling back to reveal {place} at
dawn, low mist sitting in the valley, warm sun grazing the ridgeline from camera right, slow
push and tilt up, 24mm equivalent, deep focus, 24 fps.
```

**Slots** — `{landmark}` a lone tower / a stone bridge · `{place}` a river valley / a fishing town
**Use when** the video opens and needs to establish scale in one shot.

---

### 10 · Slow lateral truck, forest

```
Lateral tracking shot through {place}, camera moving right at walking pace, trunks passing the
frame in parallax, dappled light through the canopy, 35mm, f/4, mid-morning haze, no vertical
movement, 24 fps, gentle film grain.
```

**Slots** — `{place}` a birch forest / a bamboo grove
**Use when** you need travel without a destination.

---

### 11 · Static wide, weather arriving

```
Wide static shot of {place}, storm cloud front moving in from the left, wind bending the grass,
first heavy drops hitting a puddle in the foreground, 28mm, f/8, flat grey palette with one
break of pale light on the horizon, camera locked off, 24 fps.
```

**Slots** — `{place}` an open field / a coastal road
**Use when** the mood shift is environmental rather than personal.

---

### 12 · Low-angle walk, city night

```
Low-angle tracking shot following {subject} walking away down a wet city street, camera at knee
height moving forward at the same pace, neon signs reflecting in puddles, 28mm, f/2.8, mixed
magenta and cyan practicals, 24 fps, slight lens breathing.
```

**Slots** — `{subject}` a courier with a backpack
**Use when** momentum matters more than the face.

---

### 13 · Crane up, interior to exterior

```
Camera starts at table height in {place} and cranes up and back through a window, revealing the
street beyond, late afternoon sun raking across the room, 24mm, f/5.6, dust in the light, smooth
constant-speed rise, 24 fps.
```

**Slots** — `{place}` a small diner / a workshop
**Use when** the scene has to move from intimate to public without a cut.

---

### 14 · Static long lens, heat shimmer

```
Telephoto static shot across {place}, heat shimmer distorting the horizon, a single vehicle
crossing left to right in the far distance, 200mm, f/11, hard midday sun, washed-out colours,
camera locked off, 24 fps.
```

**Slots** — `{place}` a salt flat / a desert highway
**Use when** distance and isolation are the point.

---

### 15 · Handheld follow, uneven ground

```
Handheld shot following {subject} from behind at shoulder height over {place}, frame correcting
constantly, small vertical bobs on each step, 35mm, f/2.8, overcast light, no horizon level,
24 fps, mild motion blur on the edges.
```

**Slots** — `{subject}` · `{place}` a rocky path / a market alley
**Use when** the shot has to feel unstaged.

---

### 16 · Timelapse, fixed frame

```
Fixed-frame timelapse of {place}, clouds moving fast left to right, light shifting from blue hour
to full sun, shadows sweeping across the ground, 24mm, f/8, 24 fps played back at 12x speed,
subtle flicker in the exposure.
```

**Slots** — `{place}` a rooftop water tank / a harbour crane
**Use when** you need to compress hours into two seconds.

---

**Tip** — name the end state of every move ("pulling back to reveal", "ending on the doorway").
Models will otherwise stop mid-move, which is unfixable later without a re-render.
