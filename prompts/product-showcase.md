# Product showcase

Seven templates for packshots, macro details and tabletop shots. Product prompts fail in one
specific way: the object changes shape between frames. Every template below fixes the geometry
in the first sentence and keeps the camera move to a single axis.

---

### 17 · Turntable on seamless white

```
Studio shot of {product} on a seamless white surface, camera locked off at product height, the
object rotating slowly clockwise on a turntable, three-point lighting with a soft top key and two
rim lights, 100mm, f/11, no background shadow, {surface_finish} catching the key as it turns,
24 fps.
```

**Slots** — `{product}` a cylindrical speaker · `{surface_finish}` brushed aluminium ring
**Use when** you need a clean hero loop that can also be used as a still.

---

### 18 · Macro texture pass

```
Extreme macro of {product}, focus travelling across the surface from left to right, revealing
{material_detail}, single hard light at a low grazing angle so the texture casts its own
micro-shadows, 100mm macro, f/8, stack-like sharpness, 24 fps.
```

**Slots** — `{product}` a leather wallet · `{material_detail}` stitching and grain
**Use when** the sell is material quality.

---

### 19 · Liquid pour, slow motion

```
High-speed shot of {liquid} pouring into {vessel}, thick stream breaking into droplets, splash
crown forming and collapsing, backlit against a dark background, single soft key from the right,
100mm, f/5.6, 120 fps played back at quarter speed, {palette}.
```

**Slots** — `{liquid}` cold espresso / amber syrup · `{vessel}` a glass tumbler
**Use when** appetite appeal has to be immediate.

---

### 20 · Hand-held product in use

```
Medium close-up of {subject}'s hand holding {product}, turning it slightly to show {feature},
natural window light from camera left, 50mm, f/2.8, background a plain warm wall, hand movement
small and deliberate, camera static, 24 fps.
```

**Slots** — `{product}` a folding knife · `{feature}` the locking mechanism
**Use when** scale and grip are what the buyer cannot judge from a photo.

---

### 21 · Orbit, single axis

```
Camera orbiting {product} 90 degrees from front to side at constant speed and constant radius,
product centred and stationary, soft box overhead plus a gradient grey backdrop, 85mm, f/8, no
vertical drift, 24 fps.
```

**Slots** — `{product}` a pair of headphones
**Use when** the object is symmetric enough that a half orbit reads as a full turn.

---

### 22 · Flat-lay with moving elements

```
Top-down flat-lay of {product} surrounded by {props}, camera locked off directly overhead, the
only movement is steam rising from the cup and a slow shadow shift across the surface, soft
diffused daylight, 50mm, f/8, muted palette, 24 fps.
```

**Slots** — `{product}` a ceramic mug · `{props}` a notebook, a spoon, dried leaves
**Use when** you need a still-life that feels alive without anything moving the product.

---

### 23 · Unboxing, one take

```
Single-take shot of hands opening {package} on a wooden table, camera slightly above and to the
side, hands lifting the lid and folding the paper back to reveal {product}, warm practical light
from a desk lamp, 35mm, f/4, no cut, small natural hand tremor, 24 fps.
```

**Slots** — `{package}` a kraft box · `{product}` a small device
**Use when** the shot is the first ten seconds of a product page.

---

**Tip** — describe the product's silhouette before its material. If the shape is fixed in the
prompt, the material can drift a little and still read as the same object.
